# Opportunites
The Curtius Group at Goethe University Frankfurt is open to supervising bachelor's, master's, and doctoral theses. Reach out to me, Prof Curtius, or other group members to learn more. There are substantial opportunities to get involved with our ACTRIS field measurement team and our CLOUD experimental measurement team.

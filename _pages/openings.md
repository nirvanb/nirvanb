# Opportunities
I am always open to research collaborations! Please reach out if you have a project we could work on together or just want to discuss your research!

A few more specific opportunities for collaboration are listed below.

### @Goethe University
The Curtius Group at Goethe University Frankfurt is open to supervising bachelor's, master's, and doctoral theses. Reach out to me, Prof Curtius, or other group members to learn more. There are occasional opportunities to get involved with our ACTRIS field measurement team and our CLOUD experimental measurement team.

### Taunus Observatory ACTRIS Station
The ACTRIS station at the Taunus Observatory is equipped with a wide range of trace gas and aerosol instrumentation with continous measurements. Trace gas measurements include ammonia, ozone, sulfur dioxide, NO<sub>x</sub>, alkene and aromatic volatile organic compounds, and oxidized organic compounds. Particle phase measurements include full PM<sub>1</sub> particle size distributions, bulk particle composition, black carbon mass, and aerosol forward and back scattering, as well as measurements of small ions and charged particle distributions. In addition to these online measurements, offline filter measurements are also available to assess particle composition. We have additional inlet ports available for additional instrumentation for field campaigns as well as an additional container for larger field deployments.

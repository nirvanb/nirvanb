Hello! I'm Nirvan Bhattacharyya, an atmospheric scientist studying particle formation, growth, and chemistry and trying to understand the impact of emission on human health and the climate. I am currently a postdoctoral research scientist at the [Institute for Atmospheric and Environmental Sciences](https://www.uni-frankfurt.de/111525129/Institut_f%C3%BCr_Atmosph%C3%A4re_und_Umwelt) at [Goethe University Frankfurt](https://www.uni-frankfurt.de/de). I support the [ACTRIS measurement station at the Taunus Observatory](https://nflabelling.actris.eu/facility/53) and chamber measurements for the [CLOUD project at CERN](https://home.cern/science/experiments/cloud).

My research utilizes state-of-the-science mass spectrometers to understand the chemical complexity of gasses and particles in the atmosphere, both indoors and outdoors. I marry laboratory and field measurements to understand novel emission sources and key processes. You can find my work on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=LOxv3oIAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao).

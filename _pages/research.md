# Research Areas
My work focuses on experimental atmospheric science, with measurements spanning various laboratory scales and field locations. The air is a tremendous and complex reactor, forming from individual emissions a mixture of thousands of compounds. The interplay of these emissions, both natural and human, in the earth system leads to human health and climate impacts which are difficult to predict. Simulating the air in the laboratory provides us one pathway for understanding how these emissions may form toxic gases or aerosols
